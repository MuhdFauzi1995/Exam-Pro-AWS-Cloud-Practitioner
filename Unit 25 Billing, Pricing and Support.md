## Cost by priority (Not applicable to Basic tier)
- Based on how much you pay, the extra costs take priority depending on which is larger
![[Pasted image 20261117141625.png]]
- Developer examples 
	- 1st example (Monthly spent: $500)
		- 3% x $500 = $15 (Pay $29)
	- 2nd example (Monthly spent: $1000)
		- 3% x $1000 = $30 (Pay $30)

- Business and Enterprise examples (Varies by payment)
	 - 1st example (Monthly spent: $1000)
		- 10% x $1000 = $100 (Pay $100)
	- 2nd example (Monthly spent: $5000)
		- 10% x $5000 = $500 (Pay $500)
	- 3rd example (Monthly spent: $12000)
		- Split costs into 2 parts ($10000 and $2000)
			- First part: 10% x $10000 = $1000
			- Second part: 7% x $2000 = $140
				- Total: $1000 + $140 = $1140

# AWS Marketplace
- A curated digital catalogue with thousands of software listings from independent software vendors
- Easily find, buy, test and deploy software that already runs on AWS
- Product can be free to use or have an associated charge
	- This charge becomes part of your AWS bill and once you pay, AWS Marketplace pays the provider
- The sales channel for ISVs and Consulting Partners allows you to sell your solutions to other AWS customers
