# CAPEX vs OPEX

## Overview of CAPEX (Capital Expenditure)
- Money spent upfront on physical infrastructure
- Deducting that expense from your tax bill over time
- You have to guess upfront on what you plan to spend on
![[Pasted image 20261117125115.png]]

## Overview of OPEX (Operational Expenditure)
- The costs associated with an on-premises datacenter that has shifted the cost to the service provider
- The customer only has to be concerned with non-physical costs
- You can try a product or service without investing in equipment
